This Privacy Policy explains how TradeTarget ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares personal information when you use tradetarget.in (the "Site"). By using the Site you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you sign up for our newsletter, send a content brief or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, company and the details you include in your message.

**Information collected automatically.** We may collect non-personal information about how you use the Site, such as browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and the time and date of your visit.

## How we use it

- To provide, maintain and improve the Site
- To send the emails you have signed up for
- To reply to enquiries and content briefs
- To understand how the Site is used

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information for commercial purposes. Service providers who help us run the Site or our business may process it on our behalf, and they are required to keep it confidential.

## Cookies and analytics

We use cookies to remember your preferences and to understand how people use the Site. We may use Google Analytics or similar tools, which report trends without identifying individual visitors. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.

## Calculators and search

Calculator inputs and searches run in your browser. We do not store the figures you enter.

## Third-party sites

The Site links to other websites and may show third-party products. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information.
