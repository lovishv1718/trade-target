TradeTarget covers Indian markets, IPOs, company results and personal finance for retail investors. This page explains how we work.

## Independence

Our newsroom decides what to cover. Commercial work for clients of our content services is kept separate from editorial coverage on this site. We do not accept payment to publish, change or remove a story.

## No buy or sell calls

We report and explain. We do not tell readers to buy, sell or hold a stock, fund or IPO, and we are not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst. Where a story reports a brokerage's view, we name the brokerage.

## Sources

Company results come from stock exchange filings and company releases. IPO details come from the red herring prospectus, exchange notices and the registrar. Interest rates come from the notifying body, such as EPFO, the Finance Ministry or the bank concerned. We name sources in the story wherever we can.

## Grey market premium

GMP is unofficial and unregulated. We report it as an indicator of sentiment, label it clearly and never present it as a forecast of listing price.

## Accuracy and corrections

Stories are checked by a second person before publication. If we get something wrong, we correct it promptly and note the change at the end of the story. To report an error, write to [business@tradetarget.in](mailto:business@tradetarget.in) with the link and the correction.

## Updates

Fast-moving stories, such as IPO subscription and GMP, are updated through the day. The time of the latest update is shown under the headline.

## Images

We use our own graphics and photographs licensed for reuse. Every third-party photograph is credited on the [photo credits](/photo-credits/) page.
