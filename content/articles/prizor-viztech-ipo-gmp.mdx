Prizor Viztech Limited, established in 2017, offers security and surveillance solutions including a variety of CCTV cameras for retail, government, education and infrastructure sectors. In 2022, they expanded their product range to include televisions, touch panels, and monitors, all branded under their name and made by third parties.

They also provide video management software that allows customers to manage surveillance on a single monitor and from one location. The company has supplied its products to 17 states and 2 union territories in India.

## Prizor Viztech IPO Overview

Prizor Viztech plans to launch IPO to raise ₹25.15 cr. This will be a completely new issue. IPO subscription will be open from 12 July 2024 to 16 July 2024. Allotment of shares is expected to be finalized by 18 July 2024, with the shares being listed on NSE SME on 22 July 2024. IPO price is set between ₹82 and ₹87. Company’s promoter currently holds a 93.59% stake which will decrease to 68.28% after the issue. Bigshare Services will be the registrar for the IPO.
