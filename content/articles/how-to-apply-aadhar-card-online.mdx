Getting your Aadhaar card is now easier than ever, due to the digital age we live in. You don’t need to stand in long queues or deal with lots of paperwork with the help of technology, you can apply for a new Aadhaar card from the comfort of your home. In this blog, we will guide you step-by-step on how to apply Aadhaar card online. But before we dive into that, let’s understand why the Aadhaar card is so important for people in India.

## Aadhaar Card Meaning

Aadhaar card is a unique 12-digit identification number issued by the UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India), it is like both proof of identity and address for Indian citizens, which allows individuals to access various government schemes, subsidies, and services conveniently. Aadhaar card is a mandatory document for every citizen in the country, providing not only proof of identity but also entitling the holder to a range of benefits like opening a bank account, filing income tax returns, and receiving subsidies, among others.

## How to Apply Aadhar card Online: Step by Step Process

To apply for a new Aadhaar card online, you can follow the steps outlined below.

**Step 1:** Begin by visiting the official Aadhaar website at [https://uidai.gov.in/en/](https://uidai.gov.in/en/) into your web browser.

**Step 2**: And locate the “** Get Aadhar**” section. Then, select either “** Book an Appointment**.”

**Step 3:** On the appointment page, select your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra location from the drop-down menu, and then click “** Proceed to Book Appointment**.”

<Figure src="/images/articles/how-to-apply-aadhar-card-online/inline-2.webp" width={1024} height={600} alt="Proceed to Book Appointment" />

**Step 4:** On the next page, you will find multiple options. Since we’re discussing a new Aadhar application, click on “** New Aadhar**.” Then, complete the necessary information, including your mobile number and Captcha code, and click on “** Generate OTP**.”

<Figure src="/images/articles/how-to-apply-aadhar-card-online/inline-3.webp" width={1024} height={729} alt="New Aadhar" />

**Step 5:** Confirm the OTP. This will take you to the “** Appointment Details**” page, where you will be prompted to select your Resident Type (Indian Resident/Non-Resident of India (NRI)), Application Verification Type (Document/HOF), State, City, and Aadhaar Seva Kendra. After making your selections, click **“Next**.”

**Step 6**: On the next page, you will need to provide personal details such as your name, gender, date of birth, proof of date of birth, email, as well as address details. Then click on “** Next.**“

**Step 7:** This will take you to the appointment booking page, where you can choose your preferred date and time. Click “** Next**” to proceed.

<Figure src="/images/articles/how-to-apply-aadhar-card-online/inline-6.webp" width={1024} height={415} alt="aadhar-appointment-booking" />

**Step 8:** On the next page, you will be able to review all the details you filled in earlier and then click “** Submit**” for confirmation.

**Step 9:** Visit the enrollment center and make sure to bring the original copies of documents that prove the following:

- Proof of Identity
- Proof of Address
- Proof of Birth Date

**Step 10:** When you get to the enrollment center, fill out the enrollment form with all the correct details.

**Step 11:** Submit the fully filled enrollment form and the supporting documents mentioned above. They will also take your photo and gather biometric information, including iris and fingerprint scans.

**Step 12:** The person at the center will give you an acknowledgment slip as a confirmation of your enrollment. This slip contains a 14-digit enrollment number, which you can use to check your Aadhaar card application’s status.

**Step 13:** After your information is verified, your Aadhaar card will be sent to you by mail. This might take up to 90 days (that’s 3 months) to reach you.

## How to Check Aadhaar Card Status Online?

Once you’ve applied for your Aadhaar card, you’re probably curious about when it’ll be ready. UIDAI provides an easy way to check your Aadhaar card application status online:

- Go to the UIDAI website at this link: https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/check-aadhaar and click on the "Check Aadhaar Status" option.
- Enter your enrollment number and the date/time mentioned in the acknowledgment slip.
- Fill in the security code.
- Click on the "Check Status" button.

## How to Download Aadhaar Card?

To download your Aadhaar card once it’s ready, follow these simple steps:

- Visit the website link https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/ and click on the "Download Aadhaar" section.
- Choose either "Aadhaar Number"/"Enrollment ID"/“Virtual ID” based on the info you have.
- Provide the necessary details like your name, mobile number, and security code.
- You'll get a one-time password (OTP) on your registered mobile number. Put in this OTP to continue.
- Then, you will be able to download the Aadhar card in a PDF format. To open it, you'll need to use your registered PIN code as the password.
