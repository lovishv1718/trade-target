## Advantages of Fixed Deposits (FDs)

- **Assured Returns:** FDs offer a secure investment choice with guaranteed returns, regardless of how the economy performs, making it the best choice for cautious investors.
- **Flexibility:** Offer a range of tenures, from 7 days to 10 years, that gives investors the flexibility to choose a tenure that fits their requirements.
- **Tax Benefits:** It offers tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Extra Benefits to Senior Citizens:** Banks offer higher interest rates to Senior citizens, compared to the general public, which can benefit Senior citizens during their golden years.
- **For All Investors:** FDs are suitable for all types of investors, whether you're just starting, prefer low-risk options, or are a senior citizen.

### Drawbacks Of Opening A Fixed Deposit Account

- Interest rates on FD accounts are typically lower than other investment options, such as stocks and mutual funds.
- You may have to pay a penalty. If you withdraw your money before the end of the tenure.

### How to Open a Fixed Deposit Account?

To open a fixed deposit (FD) account, you can either visit a bank branch or do it online.

#### If you are visiting a bank branch:

1. Fill out the FD application form with the required details.
2. Submit the required documents, such as your PAN card, Aadhaar card, and address proof.
3. Deposit the amount you want to invest in the FD account.
4. The bank will issue a receipt of your FD.

#### If you are opening a FD account online:

1. Log in to your net banking portal with your credentials.
2. Click on the option “Open an FD account.”
3. Fill out the required details, such as the investment amount, tenure, and nominee details.
4. Review the details before submission.
5. The bank will open the account for you and send you the details.

**_Here are some additional things to keep in mind when opening a FD account:_**

- The minimum investment amount for the FD account varies from bank to bank.
- You can choose a tenure for your account, from a few days to several years.
- You get the fixed interest rate for the entire tenure.
- You can earn higher interest rates on longer tenures.

### What Affects Fixed Deposit Interest Rates?

Before you dive into investing in Fixed Deposits, it is very important to understand what influences the interest rates.

- **Repo rate:** The repo rate is the interest rate at which banks borrow money from the central bank. When the repo rate increases, banks increase their FD rates as well. Conversely, when the repo rate decreases, banks tend to decrease their FD rates.
- **Inflation:** When inflation is high, banks tend to offer higher FD rates to compensate investors for the loss of purchasing power of their money.
- **Investment tenure:** Longer the investment tenure, the higher the interest rate that is generally offered because banks can earn more on their investments.
- **Investor profile:** Senior citizens and other special categories are often offered higher FD rates by banks.
- **Investment type:** Some banks offer special types of FDs, like tax-saving FDs and cumulative FDs, that may have different interest rates.
- **Competition:** Another important reason that affects interest rates is competition. Banks compete with each other to attract depositors, and this can also lead to variations in FD rates. Investors should compare FD rates from different banks before opening a Fixed Deposit account.
