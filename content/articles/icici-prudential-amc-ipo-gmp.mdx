## About ICICI Prudential AMC IPO

> ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company, founded in 1993, is one of India’s leading asset management companies. Its investment philosophy focuses on managing risk first while aiming to deliver steady long-term returns to investors.

As of September 30, 2025, the company manages a large Quarterly Average AUM (QAAUM) of ₹10,147.6 billion. It also offers Portfolio Management Services (PMS), Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), and advisory services to global clients.

ICICI Prudential AMC manages the highest number of mutual fund schemes in the industry a total of 143 schemes, including equity, debt, passive funds, fund-of-funds, liquid, overnight, and arbitrage schemes.

The company has a strong nationwide presence across 23 states and 4 union territories, supporting a wide and diversified investor base.

## ICICI Prudential AMC Strengths and Risks

Knowing about the strengths and weaknesses of ICICI Prudential AMC helps you make an informed decision.

### ICICI Prudential AMC IPO Strengths

- Backed by the strong reputation of ICICI Bank and Prudential Group.
- Offers a wide range of equity, debt, and hybrid mutual funds.
- Manages large and growing AUM, supported by healthy investor inflows.
- Led by an experienced management team with deep industry knowledge.

### ICICI Prudential AMC IPO Risks

- Fund performance and inflows can be affected by market volatility.
- Regulatory changes in the mutual fund industry can impact operations.
- Relies on third-party distributors for a significant part of customer acquisition.
- Faces tough competition from other AMCs offering similar products.
