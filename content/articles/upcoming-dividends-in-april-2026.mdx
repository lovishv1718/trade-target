## Summary

> **_ABB and several companies will go ex-dividend between April and May 2026, offering payouts up to ₹270 per share. Investors need to track dates carefully to be eligible for upcoming dividends in April._**

Several companies are set to trade ex-dividend in the coming week, making it important for investors looking to earn dividend income.

Between April 27 and May 27 May 2026, multiple stocks are scheduled to go ex-dividend, according to exchange data. This timeline is crucial because the ex-dividend date determines who is eligible to receive the upcoming dividend.

Only those investors who buy shares before the ex-dividend date qualify for the dividend payout. If the purchase is made on or after this date, the dividend will not be credited. The ex-dividend date falls one or two business days before the record date, which is when companies finalise the list of eligible shareholders.

## Stocks Going Ex-Dividend Next Week

- Among the names, ABB India has announced one of the highest payouts, declaring a final dividend of ₹29.59 per share. The stock will trade ex-dividend on April 30, 2026, while the record date is set for May 2, 2026.
- Stovec Industries has declared a dividend of ₹12 per share, with both the ex-dividend date and record date falling on April 30, 2026.
- 360 One WAM has announced an interim dividend of ₹6 per share and its ex-dividend and record date on April 27, 2026.
- Vesuvius India has declared a final dividend of ₹1.5 per share, with both ex-dividend date and record date set for April 30, 2026.
- Meanwhile, Alka India has announced a final dividend of ₹0.04 per share, with its record date falling on April 27, 2026.
