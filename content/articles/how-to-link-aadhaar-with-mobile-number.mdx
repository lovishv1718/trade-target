Linking Aadhaar with mobile number is important because it lets you use Aadhaar services through SMS. These services include things like securing your biometric data, getting a copy of your lost Aadhaar card, and receiving important information and OTPs on your phone.

The government made this link mandatory to stop using illegal phone numbers for unlawful activities. But don’t worry, the process is now simple. In this blog, we’ll show you how to link Aadhaar with mobile number, both online and offline. If you haven’t done this yet, keep reading to learn how.

## How to Link Aadhaar with Mobile Number Online?

_Here’s how you can link your mobile number with Aadhar Card online:_

- Go to the official Indian Postal Service website in your web browser.
- Fill in the necessary information like your name, mobile number, address, email, etc.
- From the dropdown menu, select **"PPB-Aadhaar Service."**
- Choose **"UIDAI-Mobile/Email to Aadhaar Linking/Update."**
- After filling in the required details and making the right selections, click on **"Request OTP."**
- You'll receive an OTP on your mobile number; enter it on the next screen.
- Click on **"Confirm Service Request,"** and you will be provided with a Reference Number to monitor your application's progress.
- Once your submission is completed successfully, the request will be forwarded to the nearest post office for further processing.
- An officer will visit your residence for verification. They'll use a mobile biometric device for this.
- The officer will complete the linking/update process. Keep in mind that there may be a fee for this service.

## Steps to Verify Mobile Number on Aadhar Card

Verifying your mobile number on your Aadhar card is simple if your mobile number is already registered with UIDAI. You can do this online with these steps:

- **Step 1:** Visit the UIDAI official website.
- **Step 2:** Then click on **"Verify Email/Mobile Number"** in the 'Aadhaar Services' section.
- **Step 3:** Enter your Aadhaar number and mobile number or email. You'll get an OTP (One-Time Password).
- **Step 4:** Put in the OTP and click **"Verify OTP"** to finish.

You can also do this from home; there is no need to visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre. It’s a simple way to ensure that your mobile number is correctly connected to your Aadhaar card.

## How to Link Aadhaar with Mobile Number Offline?

If you prefer to complete this process offline, you can follow few simple steps to link mobile number with aadhaar card offline.

- **Step 1:** Visit the official UIDAI website, use the mAadhaar mobile app, or call 1947 to find the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra/Aadhaar Enrolment Centre.
- **Step 2:** When you're at the Aadhaar Seva Kendra, ask for the Aadhaar Correction Form and fill it out. Make sure to provide your current phone number that you want to link to your Aadhaar.
- **Step 3:** Once you've completed the form, submit it and let the staff at the center verify your biometrics.
- **Step 4:** Once your biometrics are successfully verified, you'll receive an acknowledgment slip from the staff.
- **Step 5:** This slip will have an Update Request Number (URN), and you can use it to check your Aadhaar update status.

Once your mobile number is linked, you can use it for various Aadhaar services by entering the OTPs you receive on your phone. You can also call 1947, a toll-free number provided by UIDAI, to check the status instead of using the URN.

_\*Note you may need to pay a fee to the authorized staff at the Aadhaar Enrolment Centre for this service._

## How to Update Existing Mobile Number in Aadhaar Card?

Whether you’ve changed your phone number or need to correct an existing one, the process is simple and can be done online through the official Aadhaar portal.

- **Step 1:** Visit the official Aadhaar portal at https://ask.uidai.gov.in/.
- **Step 2:** Enter your registered phone number or email ID and fill in the captcha code. Then, click on **'Get OTP'** or **'Send OTP.'**
- **Step 3:** After receiving the One Time Password (OTP) on your registered mobile number, enter it and proceed to the next step.
- **Step 4:** From the dropdown menu labeled **"Online Aadhaar Services,"** select the option you want to change, which in this case is your mobile number.
- **Step 5:** To update your registered mobile number, select the related option and fill in the required information.
- **Step 6:** Once you've entered the captcha code, you'll receive an additional OTP on your registered mobile number. Verify this OTP and then proceed by clicking on the **"Save and Proceed"** button.
- **Step 7:** Double-check all the information for accuracy and then click **"Submit."**
- **Step 8:** You will see a Success Screen with your Appointment ID. Click on **"Book Appointment"** to schedule a visit to the nearest Aadhaar enrollment center.

After following these steps, you’ll need to visit the Aadhaar Seva Kendra in your area. There, you will be required to pay a nominal fee of Rs. 25 and provide any additional information that might be needed to complete the update.

## What are the Documents Required to Link Aadhaar with Mobile Number

To link Aadhaar with mobile number, you only need a self-attested copy of your Aadhaar card.

## What are the Charges for Linking Mobile Number with Aadhar?

When you initially enroll for Aadhaar, there are no charges to link your Aadhaar card to your mobile number.

However, if you want to link, modify, or update your mobile number in your Aadhaar records, there is a charge of Rs. 50 (including GST).

_\*Note that there is no additional cost if you make multiple changes to different fields at the same time._

## Benefits of Aadhaar Verification Mobile Number Process

Linking the mobile number to Aadhaar card offers a range of conveniences and advantages. It’s like a key that unlocks many Aadhaar-related services while keeping your personal information safe. In this age of online transactions and checks, this will make things easier, protect your data, enhance security through OTP verification, and let you access important government services smoothly.

- **Access to Aadhaar Services:** You need to link your Aadhaar card to your mobile number to use various Aadhaar-related services.
- **Online Updates:** If you want to update your Aadhaar information using the Self-Service Update Portal (SSUP), you must link your mobile number to your Aadhaar card.
- **mAadhaar App:** You can store your Aadhaar card on your mobile phone and access various Aadhaar services through the mAadhaar app.
- **e-Aadhaar:** To get your Aadhaar card online (e-Aadhaar), which is a digital version, you'll need to enter the One-Time Password(OTP) sent to your registered mobile number.
- **Enhanced Security:** OTPs are sent to your registered mobile number for added security when using Aadhaar services. These OTPs help protect your Aadhaar information.
- **No Access Without Linked Number:** If your mobile number isn't linked to your Aadhaar, you won't be able to utilize these services.
