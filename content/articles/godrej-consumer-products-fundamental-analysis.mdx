## About Godrej Consumer Products

Let’s step into the fascinating story of Godrej Consumer Share, a name that’s not just a company but a part of India’s journey. It all started with Ardeshir Godrej, lawyer turned entrepreneur who faced failures before hitting success with locks. Now, Godrej consumer products touch the lives of 1.1 billion people globally in consumer goods, real estate, appliances and more.

Back in 1920, Godrej made mark by making soap by using vegetable oil instead of animal fat. This humane approach laid the foundation for the Home & Personal Care chapter, making Godrej a top FMCG company in India.

But it doesn’t stop there, Godrej’s impact reaches international shores with almost half its FMCG earnings from Asia, Africa and Latin America. From insecticides to hair care and soaps, Godrej is a leader known for its commitment to people and the planet.

Today, Godrej is not just a brand. It’s like a story that’s a part of the everyday lives of 1.15 billion people worldwide.
