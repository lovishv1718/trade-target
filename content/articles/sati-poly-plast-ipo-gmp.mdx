Sati Poly Plast Limited, established in July 1999 makes versatile flexible packaging materials for various industries.

Between 2018 and 2019, the company doubled its production capacity from 250 to 500 tonnes per month.

Sati Poly Plast provides packaging materials to numerous states across India, including Assam, Bihar, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan among others.

Notable clients of the company include Pidilite, Adani Wilmar, and JVL.

## Sati Poly Plast IPO Overview

Sati Poly Plast is set to launch IPO to raise ₹17.36 crores. This will be an entirely new issue. Subscription for the IPO will open on 12 July 2024 and close on 16 July. Allotment of shares is expected to be finalized on 18 July 2024 and the shares are set to be listed on NSE SME on July 22, 2024. Price range for the IPO is set between ₹123 and ₹130 per share. Currently, the company’s promoter holds 86.30% stake which will be reduced post issue to 63%. The registrar for the Sati Poly IPO is Link Intime India.
