Unexpected expenses can arise at any time, no matter how well you plan your finances. In such moments, borrowing from friends or relying on credit cards may not be ideal. This is where a personal loan becomes a practical option. If you’re wondering about the personal loan meaning or what is meant by a personal loan, it’s an unsecured loan that helps you manage urgent or planned expenses at relatively lower interest rates.

Whether it’s medical needs, home repairs, debt consolidation, or a big purchase, personal loans are easy to access, require no collateral, and are often approved quickly. Before choosing one, it’s important to understand what is personal loan and how personal loans work, as personal loan impact your credit score and overall financial health.

## What Is Personal Loan?

Personal loan is a type of unsecured credit that allows you to borrow a fixed amount for almost any financial requirement, whether planned or unexpected. It offers a lump sum upfront, which you repay through monthly instalments over a set tenure.

Since no collateral is required, approval is based on your income, employment stability, credit history, and repayment capacity. Because the lender carries more risk, personal loans usually have higher interest rates than secured loans but remain popular due to their quick processing and flexible use.

However, missing repayments can affect your credit score and future borrowing ability, so it’s important to take a personal loan only when you’re confident about meeting the EMI commitments.

## How Personal Loan Works?

Personal loan works much like any other loan, where you start by applying for your desired loan and submitting the required documents to the bank after evaluating your creditworthiness. The funds will be transferred to your bank account, and you can use them as you see fit.

With a personal loan, you receive a lump sum of money that can be used for various expenses. You then repay this amount in regular installments over a set period.

If you have a fixed-rate loan, your monthly payments will remain unchanged until the loan is fully paid off. However, if you have a variable rate loan, your interest rate may change, altering the monthly amount you owe.

## Types of Personal Loans

**1. Unsecured Personal Loans**

These loans don’t require collateral or security and are approved based on your creditworthiness. You can use them for different purposes, like debt consolidation, medical expenses, or even a vacation.

**2. Secured Personal Loans**

Unlike unsecured loans, these loans are backed by collateral, like your car or savings account. Because there’s less risk for the lender, secured loans may offer lower interest rates and higher borrowing limits.

**3. Fixed-Rate Personal Loans**

The interest rate remains constant throughout the loan term, and your monthly payments remain unchanged. It provides predictability and can be easier to budget.

**4. Variable-Rate Personal Loans**

Interest rates for these loans may fluctuate based on changes in a specific financial index. This can lead to varying your monthly payments.

**5. Debt Consolidation Loans**

It helps you consolidate multiple debts into a manageable monthly payment. It can save you money on interest and simplify your financial obligations.

**6. Home Improvement Loans**

These are loans intended for home improvement projects. It can be secured or unsecured, allowing you to upgrade or repair your home.

**7. Medical Loans**

These loans cover medical expenses, including surgeries, treatments, or other healthcare-related costs not covered by insurance. Through this, you manage unexpected medical bills.

**8. Wedding Loans**

These loans cover wedding-related expenses like venue, catering, attire, etc.

**9. Education Loans**

These loans cover educational expenses like tuition, books, and living costs for college or vocational training. It can be used for yourself or for financing a child’s education.

**10. Emergency Loans**

These loans provide quick access to funds for unexpected and urgent expenses like medical emergencies or sudden repairs.

So these are some common types of personal loans in India. Remember, the availability and terms of these loans may vary depending on the lender and your financial situation. Always compare options and carefully review the terms and conditions before applying for any loan.

## What Are The Documents Required For Personal Loan In India?

Documentation varies from lender to lender. Here are the essential documents required for Personal Loan:

- Proof of Income
- Proof of Residence and Identity
- Salary slips and Form 16
- Bank statements
- Latest Income-Tax Returns
- Credit report
- Age proof (birth certificate, passport, Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, etc.)
- Photo identification proof (Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, passport, driving license, etc.)
- Address proof (Aadhaar card, passport, electricity bill, telephone bill, ration card, etc.)
