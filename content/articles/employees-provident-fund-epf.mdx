Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF) is a government-backed retirement savings scheme designed to help salaried employees build long-term financial security. It encourages disciplined savings during working years while offering tax benefits and steady returns. In this blog, we will cover what is EPF , how EPF works and EPF current interest rate.

## What is EPF?

Meaning of EPF is Employee’s Provident Fund, is a retirement savings scheme available to all salaried employees in India, administered by the Employee’s Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), which operates under the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.

Introduced in 1952, EPF aims to offer financial security to employees after retirement by requiring both the employer and the employee to contribute 12% of the employee’s basic salary and dearness allowance to the fund each month. This combined contribution allows employees to accumulate a significant savings corpus over their working years.

Current EPF interest rate is 8.25% per annum, which is higher than many traditional savings schemes. EPF interest and retirement withdrawals are tax-free, making it an effective tax-saving option. With regular monthly contributions, EPF helps employees steadily build a secure retirement corpus while enjoying long-term financial benefits.

## Benefits of EPF

> EPF isn’t just about saving money, it’s about securing your future. Here are some advantages of EPF:

- **Retirement Savings:** EPF helps build a retirement fund with contributions from both the employee and employer, offering financial security in old age.
- **Emergency Fund:** EPF serves as a safety net for unexpected expenses or emergencies, providing easy access to funds when needed.
- **Tax Benefits:** Contributions to EPF qualify for tax deductions, while the interest earned is also tax-free, offering savings up to Rs. 1.5 lakh annually.
- **Flexible Withdrawals:** Members can partially withdraw funds for various purposes like education, housing, weddings, or medical expenses.
- **Financial Protection:** EPF offers security during unemployment, disability, or death, providing support to employees and their families during tough times.
- **Pension and Insurance:** Besides the provident fund, employers contribute to the employee's pension and may also offer life insurance coverage.
- **Easy Access:** Through the Universal Account Number (UAN), members can manage their EPF accounts online, facilitating easy access and account transfers when changing jobs.

## Objectives of EPFO

Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) plays an important role in safeguarding the financial interests of employees across India. Below are the primary objective:

- **Who's Covered:** If you work in a company with at least 20 employees, you're likely eligible for EPF. Even some smaller companies can offer it if they choose to.
- **Salary Matters:** If your basic salary is up to Rs. 15,000 per month you have to contribute to EPF. But if you earn more than that you can still join voluntarily.
- **Choice is Yours:** If you make more than Rs. 15,000 you can decide if you want to sign up for EPF or not as long as you meet certain conditions.
- **Government Workers:** EPF rules don't apply to government employees, they have their own provident fund scheme.
- **International Employees:** If you are foreigner working in India and your employer follows EPF rules you might be eligible too.
- **Contract Workers:** Even if you are on contract you could still get EPF benefits and depending on some conditions set by EPFO.
- **Special Cases:** Depending on the rules,certain groups,such as apprentices and trainees,might or might not qualify for EPF

## How to Calculate EPF Interest Rate ?

Understanding how interest is calculated on your Employee’s Provident Fund (EPF) can help you better manage your EPF savings.

Here’s summary of the key points about EPF interest rates and calculations.

- Interest on EPF schemes is calculated monthly by dividing annual rate by 12.
- EPF interest rates are reviewed yearly and for the fiscal year 2024-25, it's 8.25%.
- The announced interest rate is applied to monthly balances and the total yearly interest is calculated.
- The new interest rate remains valid from April 1st of one year to March 31st of the next.
- Interest is credited to EPF accounts once a year on March 31st.
- This interest is added to next month's balance for further interest calculation.
- Accounts become dormant if no contributions are made for 36 months but inactive accounts may still earn interest for non-retired employees.
- Retired employee’s inactive accounts don't earn interest.
- Interest earned on dormant accounts is taxed based on member's income slab.
- Contributions to the Employee’s Pension Scheme don't earn interest but pension is provided after 58 years of age.

The table below lays out step-by-step calculation for the EPF interest accrued for an individual with monthly salary of Rs. 50,000 making it easy to follow and understand each component of the calculation.

| Description | Calculation | Amount (₹) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee’s Contribution (12% of Rs. 50,000) | 12% x 50,000 | 6,000 |
| Employer’s Contribution towards EPF (3.67%) | 3.67% x 50,000 | 1,835 |
| Total Contribution towards EPF Account | Employee’s contribution + Employer’s contribution | 7,835 |
| Monthly Interest Rate | (8.5 / 12)% | 0.7083% |
| Interest Accrued in One Month | Total contribution x Monthly interest rate | 55.52 |

When you contribute to the Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF), 12% of your salary goes into it. But the employer’s 12% isn’t all for EPF. It’s divided into two parts.

- **Employee contribution:** 12% of salary goes to EPF
- **Employer contribution:** 12% is split
- **3.67%** goes to EPF
- **8.33%** goes to EPS
- **EPS cap:** Calculated only on salary up to **₹15,000** per month

If you’re earning Rs. 50,000, overall, 24% of it, which is Rs. 12,000, is contributed to these schemes. Out of this, Rs. 10,750 goes to EPF, and Rs. 1,250 goes to EPS.

**_For a clearer picture:_**

In total, from your Rs. 50,000 salary, Rs. 10,750 is contributed to EPF, and Rs. 1,250 is contributed to EPS, making up the total of Rs. 12,000 going into these schemes.

## What is UAN and EPFO Portal?

> EPFO, or Employees’ Provident Fund Organization, allows all EPF subscribers to manage their PF accounts online. This means they can do things like withdraw money and check their EPF balance without needing to visit a physical office.
>
> To make this process easier, EPFO assigns each member a 12-digit number called a Universal Account Number (UAN). This number stays the same even if the employee changes jobs. So, whenever someone switches employers, their new PF account is linked to the same UAN.
>
> UAN makes it simple for members to access the EPFO portal online. However, to use these online services, employees need to activate their UAN first.

## How to Transfer EPF Funds Using Form 13

- **Update your UAN:** Ensure your Universal Account Number (UAN) is updated with your current employer.
- **Log in to EPF Member Portal:** Visit the official EPF member portal and complete the registration process if you haven't already.
- **Access Online Transfer Claim Portal:** Use your login credentials to access the Online Transfer Claim Portal within the EPF member portal.
- **Initiate Transfer Request:** Navigate to the option for requesting a transfer of funds and provide your previous employment details as required.
- **Form 13 Submission:** If you're not eligible to transfer without Form 13, fill out Form 13 with your previous employer's details.
- **Employer Authentication:** Have your previous or current employer authenticate the transfer request.
- **Receive PIN:** Upon submission, you'll receive a PIN on your registered mobile number for verification.
- **Verify and Submit**: Enter the received PIN to verify your identity and submit the transfer request.
- **Track Application:** Keep track of your transfer application using the issued tracking ID provided during the submission process.

## How to Withdraw EPF Funds Using Form 31

Form 31 allows you to withdraw money from your EPF account when required. You can apply either by visiting the EPFO office or through the EPF online portal. Below is a simple step-by-step guide to withdraw EPF money both offline and online.

### Offline EPF Withdrawal Process:

- Fill out either a 'new composite claim form' or a 'composite claim form' to start the withdrawal process.
- Submit the form to the EPFO office in your area.
- Make sure the form is attested by your employer.

#### Online EPF Withdrawal Process:

- Ensure you have an active Universal Account Number (UAN).
- Make sure your UAN-linked mobile number is active and reachable.
- Verify that your UAN is linked with Aadhaar and that you have your PAN and bank details with the IFSC code handy.
- Log into the UAN online portal & verify your KYC details.
- Follow the provided instructions to finish the withdrawal process smoothly.

## What Are EPF Withdrawal Rules?

- You can withdraw EPF completely under specific circumstances like retirement, unemployment exceeding two months, or when switching jobs with a gap of over two months.
- Partial withdrawals are allowed for purposes such as weddings, higher education, purchasing land or house construction, home loan repayment, and property renovation.

## How EPF Withdrawals Are Taxed in 2026?

- Withdrawals made before completing 5 years of employment are taxed.
- EPF investments up to Rs 1.5 lakh per year are tax-deductible under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act for both employer and employee contributions.
- Interest earned on EPF is tax-free as long as you're employed.
- Withdrawals within 5 years of opening the account are tax-free, but TDS may apply if the withdrawal exceeds Rs 50,000.
- PPF investments up to Rs 1.5 lakh per year qualify for tax credits under Section 80C. Interest and maturity amounts are tax-exempt.
