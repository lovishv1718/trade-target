Hero Motors IPO allotment is expected to be finalised on 21st Sep 2026. The issue was subscribed 7.01 times overall on Day 3. With such demand, allotment chances in Hero Motors may be lower for many investors. Investors can check their allotment status on BSE, NSE, or Kfin Technologies Ltd.

Here’s a look at the latest allotment details, Hero Motors subscription figures, GMP, and Hero Motors listing date.

## How to Check Hero Motors IPO Allotment Status

You can check Hero Motors IPO allotment status through multiple methods, such as:

**Method 1: Procedure to Check Hero Motors IPO Allotment Status Online On NSE**

- First, go to the NSE IPO bid verification link: [**https://www.nseindia.com/invest/check-trades-bids-verify-ipo-bids**](https://www.nseindia.com/invest/check-trades-bids-verify-ipo-bids)
- Now, select Equity & SME IPO bid details
- Select HeroMotors
- Enter your PAN number OR application number.
- And submit to check your allotment status.

**Method 2: Procedure to Check Hero Motors IPO Allotment Status Online On BSE**

- Visit BSE IPO allotment link: [**https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli\_check**](https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli_check)
- Now tick Equity under issue type.
- Select Hero Motors from the dropdown.
- Enter your application number OR PAN number.
- Then fill the captcha and click Search to view the allotment.

**Method 3: Procedure to check Hero Motors IPO allotment status on Kfin Technologies Ltd., through PAN Card (Registrar to the issue)**

- Go to Kfin Technologies portal: [**https://ipostatus.kfintech.com/**](https://ipostatus.kfintech.com/)
- Choose Hero Motors IPO from the dropdown list.
- Now select the PAN, Application Number, or DP ID option.
- Enter the details.
- And click Submit to see Hero Motors allotment results.

## About Hero Motors

Incorporated in April 1998, Hero Motors develops and manufactures powertrain solutions for automotive OEMs across US, Europe, India and ASEAN markets. Its products cover electric and non-electric powertrains for two-wheelers, e-bikes, electric and hybrid cars, off-road and heavy-duty vehicles.

The company operates through Powertrain Solutions and Alloys and Metallics (A&M). Its key products include CVTs, EV transmissions, electric motors, gear sets and integrated drive units.

Hero Motors serves customers such as BMW, Ducati, enviolo and HWA, along with global e-bike manufacturers. As of March 31, 2026, it had 1,388 permanent employees and 707 contract workers.
