National Stock Exchange of India IPO allotment is expected to be finalised on 22nd Sep 2026. The issue was subscribed 5.71 times overall on Day 3.

With such demand, allotment chances in NSE may be lower for many investors. Investors can check their allotment status on BSE, or MUFG Intime India Pvt.Ltd.

Here’s a look at the latest allotment details, NSE subscription figures, GMP, and NSE listing date.

## How to Check NSE IPO Allotment Status

You can check NSE IPO allotment status through multiple methods, such as:

**Method 1: Procedure to Check NSE IPO Allotment Status Online On BSE**

- Visit BSE IPO allotment link: [**_https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli\_check_**](https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli_check)
- Now tick Equity under issue type.
- Select National Stock Exchange of India from the dropdown.
- Enter your application number OR PAN number.
- Then fill the captcha and click Search to view the allotment.

**Method 2: Procedure to check NSE IPO allotment status on MUFG Intime India Pvt.Ltd., through PAN Card (Registrar to the issue)**

- Go to MUFG Intime India Pvt.Ltd. portal: [**_https://in.mpms.mufg.com/Initial\_Offer/public-issues.html_**](https://in.mpms.mufg.com/Initial_Offer/public-issues.html)
- Choose NSE IPO from the dropdown list.
- Now select the PAN, Application Number, or DP ID option.
- Enter the details.
- And click Submit to see NSE allotment results.

- If shares are allotted, shares will reflect in your demat account on 23 Sep 2026.
- If you don’t receive an allotment, the blocked amount will be released on 23 Sep 2026.
- Applicants receiving a partial allotment in NSE will get the remaining amount unblocked.
- After allotment, investors can track NSE listing-day demand and market sentiment for further cues.

## About NSE

Established in 1992, National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) is India’s largest stock exchange and one of the world’s leading multi-asset exchanges. It offers trading, clearing, settlement, listing, market data and index services across equities, derivatives, currencies, commodities, debt securities and mutual funds.

As of June 30, 2026, NSE had more than 261 million registered investor accounts, 129 million unique investors, 1,328 trading members and 3,005 listed companies. The total market capitalisation of companies listed on the exchange stood at around ₹474.08 trillion.

NSE has maintained a leading position across key market segments. As of March 31, 2026, it accounted for 99.79% of cash market turnover, 74.71% of equity futures turnover and 99.48% of equity options turnover. It also earns revenue beyond trading through businesses such as NSE Clearing, NSE Indices, NSE Data and Analytics and NSE International Exchange. These businesses provide clearing, index, market data and international trading services, giving NSE a diversified revenue base.
